About

I'm a Lending Product Manager specialised in personal loans, with a degree in Applied Statistics from Universidad Complutense de Madrid and a master's in Financial Strategy. I've spent over four years in consumer finance, where I've grown from BI intern to Data Scientist and then to my current role. That path has given me a hands-on technical foundation together with a business and product vision.

What I do now

Within the risk and BI department, I lead the cross-sell area for personal loans and I manage a team. I define the roadmap, design segmentation, propensity and risk models, and work with sales, marketing and technology to turn data into commercial decisions.

Some projects

  • A cross-sell business plan with a 2027 target 36% above the one in place when I joined.
  • Development of predictive, segmentation and risk models that drive cross-sell and upsell campaigns across the customer lifecycle.
  • An internal app built with generative AI (Claude) so the sales team can enter campaign information directly. It's still in a testing and improvement phase.
  • Automation of part of the monthly customer segmentation process for campaigns, with SQL and Python, which has noticeably reduced run time.

Tools

  • Programming: Python (Pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras), SQL, R, SAS, SPSS, MATLAB and C++.
  • Databases: SQL Server, MySQL, data modelling and ETL.
  • BI and visualization: Power BI (DAX), Tableau, Looker, SSRS, Report Builder and advanced Excel.
  • Machine learning: predictive modelling, supervised and unsupervised learning, time series, credit risk scoring, segmentation and A/B testing.
  • Cloud and tools: Azure, Azure DevOps, GitHub, Trello and generative AI (Claude).
  • Product and leadership: roadmap planning and prioritization, KPI definition, Agile (Scrum and Kanban), stakeholder and team management, and coordination of cross-functional projects.

Why this notebook

I'm applying generative AI to build internal tools and automate processes, and I'm deepening my knowledge of Data Science and generative AI for financial products. This notebook is where I organise what I learn, with my own examples and mini projects.
