Why I'm opening this notebook

metalearning

I'm studying a master's in Data Science and generative AI, and over nine months I have to publish what I learn. I could do that on autopilot. I'd rather turn it into something that's useful to me.

What I'll publish

One article for every idea worth pinning down: a concept, a mini project, or a mistake that cost me an afternoon. Each one will have its own topic and its own colour, but they share the same structure so they're easy to come back to.

The rules

  • No copy and paste. Lectures and slides are my starting point, but every article is written in my own words with examples I build myself.
  • Mini projects before loose theory. If I can't build something small with an idea, I probably haven't understood it yet.
  • Mistakes count too. What goes wrong usually teaches more than what goes right.

What this is for

First, a reference notebook: in a year I want to be able to look up how I solved something. Second, a record of my experience for anyone who wants to read it. And third, fulfilling the master's project, which is the least important reason.

The notebook will keep going after the master's ends. Whatever I learn next will find its place here too.
